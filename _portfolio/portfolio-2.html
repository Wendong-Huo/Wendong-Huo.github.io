--- title: Isogeometric boundary element methods excerpt: Transient heat conduction with heat sources using IG-DRBEM. permalink: /portfolio/portfolio-2/ share: false ---

This study develops an isogeometric dual reciprocity boundary element method for transient heat conduction with heat sources. NURBS discretization and dual reciprocity convert the problem into a system of first-order ordinary differential equations.
