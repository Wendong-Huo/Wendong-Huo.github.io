## Research question

How can local surface features improve the structural response of curved, thin-walled shells?

## Approach

The approach combines explicit MMC-based geometry descriptions, embedded spline components, and computational conformal mapping.

## Published study

The paper demonstrates topography design on plates and complex shells, including saddle, cone, torus, and vase geometries.
