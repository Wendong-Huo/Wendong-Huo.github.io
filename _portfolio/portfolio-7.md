## Research question

How can reinforcing ribs be placed efficiently on surfaces with complex geometry?

## Approach

The method couples surface parameterization, MMC-based layout optimization, and multi-patch stitching.

## Published study

The published examples include blunt, torus, flower-shaped, and tee-branch shells, with explicit designs suitable for CAD/CAE workflows.
