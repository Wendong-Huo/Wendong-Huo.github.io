## Research question

How can one explicit framework accommodate complex geometry and varying thickness without relying on shell assumptions?

## Approach

Solid moving morphable components are embedded into a curved design domain and coupled to finite-element analysis.

## Published study

The framework supports several design tasks for complex thin-walled structures. The paper provides numerical examples and discusses computational efficiency.
